Privacy Policy for Perch

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Perch ("we," "our," or "us") is built to help you locate public park benches and outdoor seating. Your privacy is central to how the app works.

1. Location Information

When you grant permission, Perch accesses your device's approximate or precise location (GPS) solely while you are using the app to center the map, display nearby benches, and calculate walking distances. Location coordinates are processed on your device and are not stored on our servers, sold, or used for advertising.

2. Third-Party Map & Navigation Services

To render map tiles and provide turn-by-turn walking directions when you tap to navigate, Perch connects to third-party mapping services (such as Google Maps / OpenStreetMap tile providers). Standard network requests (such as your IP address and requested map region) are transmitted to those providers in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

3. Diagnostics & Data Retention

Perch does not require account registration and does not collect your name, email address, or personal profile. Basic, anonymized crash diagnostics provided through Google Play Services may be used to maintain app stability.

4. Your Choices

You can revoke location permissions at any time through your Android device's system settings. Perch will remain usable for manual map browsing even if location access is denied.
